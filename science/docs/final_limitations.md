# Final limitations

1. The DiffQP backward path was not activated in the formal literature-calibrated learning regime because every registered sample failed LICQ. Regular-case validation establishes implementation correctness, not empirical learning benefit.
2. Nominal hard-action feasibility was zero and QP intervention was 100% for both formal-learning branches.
3. Predictive early-warning support is limited to the frozen targeted boundary-approach family. A negative predictive certificate is otherwise an inconclusive conservative warning.
4. Age-delayed recursive communication produced missed anticipatory triggers and is not the instantaneous centralized fleet minimum.
5. The hard switch between low-speed ZOH and thermal-HOCBF sufficient conditions is not continuous at `v_epsilon` in the registered hot-brake case.
6. The pre-registered numerical string-stability criterion was not satisfied in the grade-transition cases.
7. The vehicle is a cross-source, literature-calibrated, physics-based model. It is not an identified real truck, field validation, or hardware-in-the-loop validation.

Additional claim boundaries: M6 contains certificate losses, so no universal robustness statement is made; the predictive guarantee is finite-horizon and conditional on the registered model and uncertainty set, not global recursive feasibility; and M10 timing is platform-specific, not target-hardware certification.

These limitations are stated explicitly in the Abstract where material, the Results/Discussion section, and the Conclusion.
