## Defensible contribution boundary

The paper integrates a complete exact certificate and a backup-compatible robust predictive monitor for a specific heavy-truck conflict: collision demand competes with thermally degrading friction braking and lagged, gear/power-limited auxiliary braking under zero-order-held commands, communication uncertainty, heterogeneous dynamics and fleet propagation. The critical future vehicle, step and physical limit are used for anticipatory decentralized cooperation. A differentiable surrogate trains the actor but is kept outside the safety theorem.

This audit used official IEEE/Elsevier records where accessible and primary arXiv records for the two 2026 preprints. It is a bounded review through the date above, not a claim that no additional paper exists.
